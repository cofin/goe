# Task 1.3: Rewire predicate_offload.py and dialect predicate callers onto goe.offload.predicates and remove lark

## Objective

1. Replace the 868-LOC Lark pipeline in `src/goe/offload/predicate_offload.py` with a thin ~60-LOC module backed by `goe.offload.predicates` (`GenericPredicate` wrapping `PredicateExpr`, `create_and_relation_predicate`, `create_or_relation_predicate`, `python_timestamp_to_string`, `parse_safe_sql_predicate`, `PredicateOffloadException`, and `get_predicate_dialect_engine`).
2. Update `PredicateDialectEngineProtocol` in `src/goe/offload/sql_engine.py` to accept `PredicateExpr` (keeping `sql_engine.py` free of `sqlglot` imports).
3. Rewire `bigquery_predicate.py`, `oracle_predicate.py`, `snowflake_predicate.py`, `synapse_predicate.py`, `hadoop_predicate.py`, and `spark_thrift_predicate.py` to compile `GenericPredicate.expr` through `resolve_predicate_types` and `render_predicate`, deleting their Lark `Transformer` classes (`TypedPredicateToSnowflakeTemplate`, `TypedPredicateToSynapseTemplate`, `TypedPredicateToHadoopTemplate`, `TypedPredicateToSparkThriftTemplate`, etc.).
4. Remove `"lark>=1.1.9"` from `pyproject.toml` and update `tests/unit/offload/test_predicate_offload.py`.

## Context

- `src/goe/offload/predicate_offload.py` is imported by:
  - `src/goe/offload/bigquery/bigquery_predicate.py`
  - `src/goe/offload/oracle/oracle_predicate.py`
  - `src/goe/offload/snowflake/snowflake_predicate.py`
  - `src/goe/offload/microsoft/synapse_predicate.py`
  - `src/goe/offload/hadoop/hadoop_predicate.py`
  - `src/goe/offload/spark/spark_thrift_predicate.py`
  - `src/goe/offload/offload_source_table.py`, `src/goe/offload/backend_table.py`, `src/goe/offload/offload_messages.py`, `src/goe/offload/offload_metadata_functions.py`, `src/goe/offload/operation/data_type_controls.py`, `src/goe/offload/operation/partition_controls.py`, and `src/goe/persistence/orchestration_metadata.py` (which construct `GenericPredicate(dsl_str)` and call `.dsl`, `.column_names()`, `.alias_column_names()`, `.rename_column()`, `.set_column_alias()`, `create_and_relation_predicate()`, `create_or_relation_predicate()`, and `python_timestamp_to_string()`).
- Keeping `GenericPredicate` as a thin wrapper around `PredicateExpr` (`self.ast: PredicateExpr`) preserves every caller contract in `src/goe/offload/` while eliminating all Lark `Tree` / `Token` / `Transformer` classes and removing the `lark` dependency from `pyproject.toml`.
