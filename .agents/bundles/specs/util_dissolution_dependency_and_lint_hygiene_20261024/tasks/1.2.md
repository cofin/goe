## Objective

1. Relocate `src/goe/util/password_tools.py` and `src/goe/util/gcp_credentials.py` to `src/goe/config/password_tools.py` and `src/goe/config/gcp_credentials.py` (preserving 100% binary compatibility with AES-256-CBC + PKCS7 `$OFFLOAD_HOME` keyfiles and stripping the 31-line `__main__` demo block in `password_tools.py`).
