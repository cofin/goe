# Task 1.7: Remove the Sqoop, MapRFS, and Teradata configuration surface

## Objective

1. Remove the Sqoop, MapRFS, and Teradata constants from `src/goe/offload/offload_constants.py`, the MapRFS scheme handling from `src/goe/filesystem/goe_dfs.py`, `src/goe/filesystem/cli_hdfs.py`, and `src/goe/util/hive_ddl_transform.py`, and the Sqoop, MapRFS, and Teradata options, defaults, config fields, validation branches, CLI flags, and Listener schema fields.
2. Delete `templates/conf/offload.env.template.teradata`, remove Teradata build rules from `templates/conf/Makefile`, remove Sqoop and MapRFS settings from `templates/conf/offload.env.template.hadoop`, and remove the `teradata` optional dependency extra from `pyproject.toml`.
3. Verify that zero Sqoop, MapRFS, or Teradata references remain anywhere in `src/`, `tests/`, `templates/`, or `pyproject.toml`.
